MEXA Leaderboard

Multilingual Evaluation of English-Centric LLMs via Cross-Lingual Alignment

MEXA assesses the multilingual capabilities of English-centric LLMs. These models use English as a kind of pivot language in their intermediate layers, so MEXA computes the alignment between each language and English on parallel sentences, and uses it to estimate how much of the model's English task performance transfers to that language. Higher is better.

ARC style: mean pooling over layers, scaled by the model's English ARC score. Belebele style: max pooling over layers, scaled by its English Belebele score. Average: the mean of the two. Parallel data: FLORES-200 (first 100 devtest sentences) or the Bible (103 sentences).
